    Resolved mode (prototype)¶

    ceridwen.resolved fits band images instead of integrated photometry. Every cell of a pixel grid carries its own stellar population. The cells are rendered through each band's PSF into the images, and all cells are fitted under one image likelihood with a spatial prior. It is a prototype: the pieces below exist, are differentiable and tested, and have been validated on one mock. It is not a production pipeline.

    What exists¶

    Piece What it does
    CellGrid(csp, phot, shape, psfs, zred=...) Per-cell logmass (formed), logsfr_ratios on the CSP's shared lookback bins (step SFH, one SFR per bin), diffuse_tau_kc; one logzsol for the galaxy; fixed redshift. cell_maggies(theta) gives (n_cells, n_bands) AB maggies, render(theta) gives (n_bands, n_y, n_x) images.
    GaussianMixturePSF(amplitudes, sigmas_arcsec) PSF as a mixture of circular Gaussians, integrated over the pixel. nircam_psfs(filters) gives one Gaussian per NIRCam band with the JDox FWHM (F115W 0.040", F150W 0.050", F200W 0.066", F277W 0.092", F356W 0.116", F444W 0.145").
    MRFPrior(weight) First-difference Gaussian Markov random field on each cell-parameter map: -0.5 * weight * sum |grad theta|^2, one weight per parameter or one for all.
    resolved_map_fit(model, images, sigmas, prior) MAP by L-BFGS (optax, as ceridwen.optimize.map_fit) on the unconstrained parameters with jax.grad. It returns the MAP theta, the maps (mass, SFR10, SFH ratios, dust), the totals, the number of steps and gradient evaluations, the compile and run times, and a logmass constraint map.

    How the model is built:

    • No second SPS. A cell is CSPBasis.predict(theta_cell, [phot]) for a fixed-redshift Photometry, the same call fitSED makes for one galaxy, mapped over the cells with jax.lax.map (batch_size cells at a time). Each cell is predicted at logmass = 8 and then scaled in float64 by 10**(logmass - 8). This is exact, because predict is linear in the mass. It is needed because the CSP forms the observed F_nu in float32, which underflows to zero below about 100 M_sun at z = 6.
    • Image. Each cell is a point source at its centre, and pixel = cell. The image of band b is sum_k a_k Ky_k F_b Kx_k^T, where the K matrices are the Gaussian integrated over each pixel. The convolution is exact up to the grid edge (flux falling off the grid is lost, as on a detector), differentiable, and free of FFT wrap-around. The second moment of a point source is sqrt(sigma^2 + d^2/12) per axis.
    • Likelihood. Diagonal Gaussian over the images, with one sigma per band or one per pixel. An optional mask (True means the pixel is used) may cover NaN pixels.
    • Priors. Per-cell priors (default_cell_priors()): logmass Uniform(0, 11), logsfr_ratios StudentT(0, 1, 2), diffuse_tau_kc ClippedNormal(0.3, 1, 0, 4), logzsol Uniform(-2, 0.2). The MRFPrior adds the spatial term on top of these.
    • Constraint map. constraint_ratio is, per cell, the Gauss-Newton curvature of the likelihood in logmass divided by the prior curvature (the GMRF precision diagonal). For a one-dimensional Gaussian, KL(posterior || prior) = 0.5 [ln(1 + r) - r/(1 + r)] plus a mean-shift term. This ratio r is the MAP, per-cell, diagonal version of that KL divergence.
    from ceridwen.resolved import CellGrid, MRFPrior, resolved_map_fit
from ceridwen.resolved.psf import nircam_psfs

csp = CSPBasis(ssp, lookback_time=[0.0, 0.02, 0.1, 0.6, 0.9], sfh_per_bin=True,
               sfh_interp="step", zh_const=True, add_diffuse_dust=True, add_neb=True,
               gas_tied=True, add_igm=True, cosmo=Cosmology.planck18())
filters = ["jwst_f115w", "jwst_f150w", "jwst_f200w", "jwst_f277w", "jwst_f356w", "jwst_f444w"]
model = CellGrid(csp, Photometry(filters=filters, name="nircam"), (64, 64),
                 nircam_psfs(filters), zred=6.0, pixel_scale=0.03)
res = resolved_map_fit(model, images, sigmas, MRFPrior(1.0), max_steps=800)
print(res.summary())

    examples/resolved/resolved_mock.py is the complete worked example: it builds the mock, runs the held-out choice of the prior weight, and runs both fits.

    What does not exist¶

    • No sampler. Only the MAP is available. There are no posterior widths and no evidence.
    • No exposure-level rendering. The model is fitted to mosaic-like images on the cell grid. There are no per-exposure WCS, distortion or drizzle, no sub-pixel cell offsets, and no sky or background terms.
    • No sampled correlation length. The MRF weight is fixed. The example picks one global weight by held-out predictive likelihood.
    • No real PSFs. Only Gaussian mixtures are available (no WebbPSF/STPSF models, no field-dependent PSF, no PSF uncertainty).
    • One metallicity and one redshift per galaxy. Per-cell dust is the diffuse Kriek & Conroy optical depth only, at the CSP's default dust index.
    • Noise is uncorrelated. Pixel-to-pixel noise correlations (e.g. from drizzling) are not modelled.
